B.A.S.D. · Summer 2026

Privacy-aware teacher feedback analysis

Structuring qualitative evaluation comments while limiting identity exposure.

Research question

How can inconsistent evaluation records and free-text comments support useful comparisons across schools?

Approach

Standardize 50,000+ records, extract themes from 25,000+ comments with Claude Haiku and Sonnet, and compare patterns in Power BI.

My contribution

Built data preparation and text analysis workflows, including spaCy NER, rule-based PII masking, and separate identity mappings.

Evaluation & evidence

The lowest-scoring evaluation area also had the most missing supporting evidence. Findings were presented to inform teacher training and support priorities.

Project context

Completed summer internship. This case study summarizes the methods and aggregate findings.
