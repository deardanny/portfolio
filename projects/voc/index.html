Finding themes in Korean customer complaints

Turning recurring support conversations into interpretable complaint themes.

Korean text processing & topic modeling
Scope
Support tickets · Feb 2022–Feb 2023
When
2023

The question

Which recurring themes are difficult to see when customer-service tickets are reviewed one at a time?

Approach

The pipeline parses dates, cleans text, and uses KoNLPy’s Okt analyzer to extract Korean tokens. CountVectorizer represents unigrams and bigrams with domain stopwords. Separate LDA models within complaint categories produce topic mixtures, which can be inspected in pyLDAvis and assigned back to tickets.

My contribution

Developed the preprocessing and topic-modeling workflow. I used category-level models and vocabulary filtering to make outputs interpretable, then connected topic assignments to category and time-based exploration.

Results & evidence

The public notebook documents the methodology, including six topics per complaint category and highest-probability topic assignment. This is a distinct analysis of support tickets, complementing the review and social-post monitoring platform.

Scope & interpretation

Topic labels require interpretation and are not ground-truth classes. Phone-ticket summaries may omit useful detail. Public notebook outputs were cleared to protect company data; the illustrated example is synthetic and contains no measured topic frequencies.

What this work taught me

Useful topics depend as much on text collection and category structure as on the model.
