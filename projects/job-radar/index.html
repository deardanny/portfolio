Method & project evidence Open the supporting figure

Approach

Adapters collect postings from platforms including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and SmartRecruiters. The pipeline normalizes descriptions and dates, checks role and eligibility criteria, and compares postings with résumé variants using TF-IDF similarity and skill coverage. LLM-assisted reporting turns qualified results into readable summaries.

My contribution

Built and iterated a personal workflow spanning collection, filtering, comparison, deduplication, and Discord reporting, using AI-assisted development. Added regression tests for date provenance, sponsorship wording, recommendation gates, and which URLs are recorded as already reported.

Results & evidence

The implementation handles missing evidence, keeps verification-needed results separate, and persists state. A posting can be withheld despite a strong text match when its date or eligibility evidence is insufficient.

Scope & interpretation

Similarity is an internal discovery aid, not an ATS score or a probability of an offer. Recommendations depend on posting evidence and require human review. Private profiles and integration credentials are not included here.

What this work taught me

Abstaining when evidence is missing can matter more than ranking every candidate.
