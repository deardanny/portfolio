CMU · Research

LLM-assisted literature screening

Adding a human–AI agreement check to systematic literature review.

Research question

How can an LLM provide a systematic second check on human decisions when screening research papers?

Approach

Compare LLM inclusion decisions with human-assigned labels, measure agreement with Cohen’s kappa, and route low-confidence cases for manual review.

My contribution

Developed the LLM screening classifier and evaluation workflow as part of research on human–AI collaboration.

Evaluation & evidence

Approximately 0.80 Cohen’s kappa against human-assigned labels. This measures agreement, not classification accuracy or proof that every shared decision is correct.

Project context

A second layer of screening supports human review. Low-confidence LLM decisions are routed to reviewers.
