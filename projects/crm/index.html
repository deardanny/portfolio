Customer modeling & targeting experiments

Taking churn and purchase propensity models into a measurable CRM experiment.

Industry projectLightGBMHyperoptMLflowA/B testing
My role
Predictive modeling & campaign evaluation
Scope
Approximately 14,000 test participants
When
2021 — 2025

The question

Does model-based audience selection improve engagement compared with randomly selected customers receiving the same type of offer?

Approach

I developed LightGBM churn and purchase propensity models with more than 20 behavioral and lifecycle features. Hyperopt supported tuning and MLflow tracked experiments in Databricks. Customer scores informed audience selection, while Power BI reporting supported ongoing CRM planning.

My contribution

Built customer models and worked with app marketers on an A/B test covering approximately 14,000 users. I connected predictive scores to coupon targeting and helped evaluate the resulting engagement.

Results & evidence

The best-performing model-targeted segment achieved 42% higher click-through rate than its randomly targeted comparison group. Both groups received coupons, making the comparison about audience selection rather than whether an offer was sent.

Scope & interpretation

The 42% increase is relative and applies to the strongest segment, not the average across all participants. The visualization indexes the comparison group to 100; it does not imply an absolute CTR of 100% or 142%.

What this work taught me

An audience model earns its place through the decisions and experiments it supports, not a training metric alone.
