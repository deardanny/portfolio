Kimberly-Clark · Applied ML

Customer modeling & targeting experiments

Connecting churn and purchase propensity models to CRM decisions.

Research question

Does model-based audience selection improve engagement compared with randomly selected coupon recipients?

Approach

Build LightGBM models with 20+ behavioral and lifecycle features; use Hyperopt for tuning and MLflow for experiment tracking on Databricks.

My contribution

Developed customer-level models and automated monthly Power BI reporting; worked with marketers on an A/B test covering approximately 14,000 users.

Evaluation & evidence

The best-performing model-targeted segment achieved 42% higher click-through rate than its random-targeting comparison group. Both groups received coupons.

Project context

The 42% relative CTR increase applies to the best-performing model-targeted segment within the approximately 14,000-user experiment.
