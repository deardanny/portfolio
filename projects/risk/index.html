NLP for product issue monitoring

Connecting large-scale consumer feedback to product-risk monitoring and action.

Industry projectGPT-4oSnowflakeDatabricksPower BI
My role
In-house platform development
Scope
20,000+ daily reviews and social posts
When
2021 — 2025

The question

How can product teams find emerging issues in thousands of daily reviews without reading every post themselves?

Approach

The platform combines sentiment classification, risk-keyword monitoring, brand and competitor comparisons, and review summaries. Snowflake and Databricks support processing, Azure Data Factory orchestrates the workflow, and Power BI lets marketing and manufacturing teams inspect signals and trace them back to feedback.

My contribution

Led development of the in-house platform and the transition from a vendor solution. I developed classification workflows, tested the move from RoBERTa to GPT-4o API prompting, and connected outputs to dashboards, manuals, and user training.

Results & evidence

Sentiment classification achieved 95% accuracy on human-labeled reviews, compared with 80% for RoBERTa and 60% for the vendor system. The workflow covered 100+ products and 20,000+ daily reviews and posts, with approximately 90% lower annual platform costs.

Scope & interpretation

Issue identification and notification to manufacturing shortened from approximately 90 days to 3 days. This describes detection and handoff, not full issue resolution. Sentiment accuracy is not the recall of every product-risk keyword.

What this work taught me

The value of text classification comes from getting a useful signal to the person who can act on it.
