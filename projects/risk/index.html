Kimberly-Clark · Industry

NLP for product issue monitoring

Turning consumer reviews into sentiment, risk signals, and concise summaries.

Research question

How can product teams identify emerging issues within large volumes of consumer feedback?

Approach

Combine GPT-4o sentiment classification and prompt design with keyword monitoring, review summaries, and Power BI reporting. The pipeline processes 20,000+ reviews and social posts daily across 100+ products.

My contribution

Led development of the in-house NLP platform, replacing a vendor solution and connecting text analysis to marketing and manufacturing workflows.

Evaluation & evidence

95% sentiment accuracy on human-labeled reviews, compared with 80% for RoBERTa and 60% for the vendor system. Annual platform costs decreased by approximately 90%.

Project context

Issue identification and notification to manufacturing decreased from approximately 90 days to 3 days.
