Human–AI collaboration in Overcooked

Designing shared tasks that make coordination with an AI teammate observable.

Ongoing researchHuman–AI teamingExperiment designLLM prompting
My role
Task design, environment changes & prompting
Scope
Collaborative game experiment
When
2026 — present

The question

How do people divide responsibilities, communicate, and adapt their strategies while working with an AI partner?

Approach

The study uses an existing Overcooked-style environment as a shared task. I modified kitchen layouts and rewards to create more meaningful choices about task allocation, and refined the LLM prompts used in the interaction. Different recipe rewards give coordination a concrete consequence in the game.

My contribution

Changed game maps, implemented onion-soup and tomato-soup rewards of 20 and 30 points, refined prompts, and contributed to experimental design. My work extends an inherited research platform and connects implementation decisions to the behaviors the study aims to observe.

Results & evidence

The current deliverable is a modified experimental environment and task design. It supports investigating coordination and adaptation; completed participant-study results are not yet available.

Scope & interpretation

Recipe rewards are experimental settings, not measured improvements in participant performance. The LLM component is prompting; no separate document-retrieval system is claimed.

What this work taught me

A useful research platform needs tasks that reveal differences in behavior, not just a game that runs.
