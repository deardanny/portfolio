CMU · Ongoing research

Human–AI collaboration in Overcooked

Designing tasks, rewards, and prompts to study coordination with AI teammates.

Research question

How do people coordinate roles and adapt their strategies while working with an AI teammate?

Approach

Adapt an existing Overcooked research environment by changing layouts, reward structures, and the prompts given to LLM teammates.

My contribution

Modified the game maps, introduced different rewards for onion and tomato soup, refined LLM prompts, and contributed to experimental design.

Evaluation & evidence

Implemented task and environment modifications to support a planned study of role coordination and strategy adaptation.

Project context

Ongoing experimental preparation. Participant-study results are not yet available.
